## Lessons Learned

* RSTP does not use the Blocking, Listening, and Disabled states separately; they are combined into the Discarding state.
* Port **roles** and port **states** are different concepts. A role explains *why* a port exists, while a state explains *what* the port is currently doing.
* The Root Bridge is elected using the lowest Bridge ID.
* RSTP achieves much faster convergence than traditional STP by reducing unnecessary waiting states and using rapid handshakes.

## Challenges

* Needed additional practice identifying Root, Designated, and Alternate ports from a topology instead of memorizing definitions.
